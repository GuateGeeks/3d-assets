7. `gg3d-blender-web` must retain its cotizador names, pivots, budgets and pose sampling rules. General animation belongs in a separate route.
8. Rivello/LPM adapters correctly subordinate upstream recipes to local budgets and QA. Preserve their source locks and documented script limitations; do not enable their unrelated paid services.

`model-profiles.json` is routing preference, not model enforcement or attestation. `benchmark-plan.json` remains NOT_RUN with paid providers disabled. Provider configuration should be distinct from author/reviewer model preferences and benchmark authorization. Project MCP examples contain portable placeholders; actual paths/configuration remain local. Do not unify bpy 4.2 and native Blender 5.x environments by upgrading one to match the other.

## Provider and animation interoperability

Separate provider capabilities, transport capabilities and implemented repository adapters. Tool discovery alone establishes none of authentication, funding or successful execution.

- [Meshy rigging](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/rigging) accepts suitable textured humanoid bipeds facing +Z. The API documents 320,000 faces; the exposed native MCP restricts to 300,000. Enforce the selected transport's stricter limit.
- [Meshy animation](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/animation) requires a Meshy rig task ID. A UniRig or Tripo GLB cannot be substituted for that ID.
- [Meshy text-to-motion](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-motion) offers standalone motion; applying it to a different skeleton requires a separate retargeting step.
- [Tripo's schema](https://platform.tripo3d.ai/docs/schema) separates prerig check, rig and retarget; body type and version constrain available motion. Magnific's animation transport must not be presented as Tripo retargeting.
- [UniRig](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/UniRig) generates skeletons and weights; it is not a motion generator. Review predicted skeletons before expensive downstream work.
- [SkinTokens](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/SkinTokens) supports skeleton/weight inference and existing-skeleton options, but documents at least 14 GB NVIDIA VRAM. Configure a separate eligible executor before claiming support.

Identical GLB/FBX containers do not imply compatible bone semantics, rest pose, bind matrices, units, axes or root motion. Rig, motion and retargeted output need distinct identities and QA.

Costs depend on provider and transport; do not equate credit units. Persist requests before submission and task IDs immediately afterward. Resume known tasks instead of repeating an uncertain paid submission. Download authorized outputs promptly: [Meshy retention](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/asset-retention) makes remote URLs unsuitable as permanent evidence.
