## Rights and exclusions

- Original geometry, built from procedural components. No mesh, texture or image from any third party was imported or traced.
- Reference photos of the real board were used for proportions only. They are not textures, are not included in this repository and are not redistributed.
- The model carries no BBC or micro:bit logo, no FCC ID and no silkscreen text; the touch logo is a plain gold ring with two dots. "micro:bit" names the board it resembles and implies no endorsement.

## Dimensions and estimates

- Real hardware (V2.2 revision, https://tech.microbit.org/hardware/2-2-revision/): 51.60 x 42.00 x 11.65 mm including components.
- This model: 51.60 mm along X, 11.65 mm tall (Y), and 43.25 mm along Z, because the USB connector overhangs the 42.00 mm board edge. Units are metres and nothing is rescaled implicitly.
- Estimated, not taken from the specification: PCB thickness (1.6 mm assumed), every component position and size (read off photos), the colours and material roughness.

## Known non-blocking defects (from the independent review of the run)

1. The edge connector has 14 gold fingers; the real board has about 20 narrower ones, including some outside pins 0 and GND.
2. A small dark component on the back, between the reset button and the battery socket, touches the reset housing and the socket wall (possible intersection).
3. The gold ring around each pin hole is about 0.6 mm wide, thinner than the real rings (about 1 mm).

## Orientation and node contract

Studio / glTF: Y up, LED face +Y, edge connector toward +Z. Nodes are resolved by `extras.part_id` per `node-contract.json` (46 parts, 25 LEDs `led_x_y` with x = column and y = row, origin top-left, each with its own material).

## Demo scene

`microbit.scene.json` is generated by `studio/web/scripts/generate-microbit-scene.mjs` (a copy for the dev server is written to `studio/web/public/microbit/`). Its `patternBindings` (A heart, B smile, logo check) and its LED `tracks` are authored stand-ins for a future live feed from the MakeCode simulator. They are not simulator output, not a validation of MakeCode behaviour, and use only the declarative `patternBindings` and `tracks` of the scene format.

## Status of this evidence

Visual and content judgements above belong to the lab review of the Blender renders. The Studio captures in `studio/web/evidence/microbit-20261031/` are functional evidence from software rendering; GPU performance is `NOT_RUN`, as is any live MakeCode simulator wiring.
